# Small Commercial Combined — Appetite

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · guide UWC-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Target business

Retail shops, offices, light manufacturing, tradesmen and small property owners with a
turnover up to £5,000,000 and a maximum sum insured of £2,500,000 at any one location.

## 2. Employers liability

Employers liability is written at £10,000,000 as standard and is not written on a
standalone basis.

## 3. Public liability

Public liability limits of £1,000,000, £2,000,000 and £5,000,000 are available. Any
limit above £5,000,000 is referred to the underwriting manager.

## 4. Working at height

Trades working at or above two storeys are referred. Roofing contractors, scaffolders
and steeplejacks are outside appetite.

## 5. Business interruption

Business interruption is written on a gross profit basis with a minimum indemnity
period of 12 months. An indemnity period below 12 months is not offered, because it
is the single most common cause of an underinsured commercial claim.

## 6. Surveys

A survey is required where the total sum insured exceeds £1,000,000, where the trade
involves heat processes, or where the property is Grade II listed.
