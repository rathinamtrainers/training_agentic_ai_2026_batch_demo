# Declined Risks

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · reference UWD-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose

The list of risks Northwind does not write on any product. It exists so that a broker
gets a straight answer in one call rather than a week of referrals.

## 2. Motor

Drivers under 25 on group 30 or above vehicles. Hire and reward including private
hire and food delivery. Any vehicle imported outside the EU without a UK type
approval. Any proposer with an unspent conviction for insurance fraud.

## 3. Home

Prefabricated reinforced concrete of the Cornish, Airey or Woolaway types. Properties
in flood band D for new business. Properties with unresolved subsidence. Properties
used for commercial cannabis cultivation, which is named here because it has happened
twice.

## 4. Commercial

Waste and recycling operations. Fireworks manufacture or storage. Nightclubs. Any
business with a turnover above £5,000,000, which belongs to the corporate market and
not to Northwind.

## 5. Reviewing the list

The list is reviewed annually by the underwriting manager with the reinsurance
broker. Nothing is added or removed inside the year without board approval.
