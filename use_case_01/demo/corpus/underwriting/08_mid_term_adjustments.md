# Mid-Term Adjustments

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · procedure UWM-06 · reviewed 2026-05-12

## 1. What can be adjusted

Address, vehicle, drivers, sums insured, cover level and optional extensions may be
adjusted mid-term. The product itself cannot be changed; that is a cancellation and a
new policy.

## 2. Administration fee

A mid-term adjustment fee of £25 is charged for each adjustment, except where the
adjustment is the correction of a Northwind error or the addition of cover following
a life event such as bereavement.

## 3. Re-underwriting

An adjustment that brings the risk into a referral category re-underwrites the whole
policy, not just the change. An adjustment that brings it into a declined category
means the policy is cancelled from the date of the change with pro-rata return of
premium.

## 4. Return premiums

Return premiums under £15 are not issued and the customer is told at the point of the
adjustment.

## 5. Effective date

An adjustment takes effect from the date the customer tells the broker, not the date
it is keyed. Backdating beyond 30 days requires a senior underwriter.
