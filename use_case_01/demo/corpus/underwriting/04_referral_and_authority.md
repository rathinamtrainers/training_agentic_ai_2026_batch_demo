# Referral and Underwriting Authority

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · reference UWA-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Underwriter authority

An underwriter may quote and bind within the published appetite up to a premium of
£5,000 and a sum insured of £750,000 without referral.

## 2. Senior underwriter authority

A senior underwriter may bind up to £25,000 premium, may agree a rate deviation of up
to 15% from the technical rate, and may accept one referral criterion outside
appetite with a written rationale.

## 3. Underwriting manager

Anything above those limits, any deviation over 15%, and any risk with two or more
referral criteria, requires the underwriting manager.

## 4. Declines are not referrals

Where this guide says a risk is declined, it is declined. It is not a referral, and
an underwriter who refers it is asking a colleague to breach the appetite rather than
to exercise judgement.

## 5. Recording a rationale

Every referral decision carries a written rationale in Polaris naming the criterion,
the evidence relied upon and the terms applied. Portfolio reviews read these; an
empty rationale is treated as an unauthorised acceptance.
