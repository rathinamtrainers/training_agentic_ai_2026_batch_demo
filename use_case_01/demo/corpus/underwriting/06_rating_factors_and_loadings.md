# Rating Factors and Loadings

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · reference UWR-03 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Motor rating factors

Vehicle group, postcode band, driver age, licence type and years held, claims and
convictions in five years, annual mileage, overnight parking, and use class.

## 2. Home rating factors

Postcode band, flood band, property age and construction, number of bedrooms, rebuild
cost, occupancy, security, and claims in five years.

## 3. Standard loadings

Unoccupancy 25%. Non-standard construction 20%. Previous escape of water claim within
three years 15%. Business use from home 10%. Loadings are applied multiplicatively in
that order.

## 4. Discounts

No claims discount up to 65% at nine or more years. Combined buildings and contents
10%. Approved alarm 7.5%. No discount is applied to a risk accepted outside standard
appetite.

## 5. Minimum premium

The minimum premium is £150 for home and £275 for motor, net of insurance premium
tax. A quotation below the minimum is uprated automatically and the broker is told
why.
