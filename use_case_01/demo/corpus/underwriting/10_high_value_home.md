# High Value Home Referral

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · procedure UWH-09 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. What counts as high value

A home risk with a rebuild cost above £750,000, contents above £150,000, or any
single specified item above £25,000.

## 2. Referral route

High value home risks are referred to the private clients underwriter and are never
quoted from the standard rating engine.

## 3. Survey requirement

A survey by an approved surveyor is required before inception where the rebuild cost
exceeds £1,500,000 or where the property is listed. The survey is at Northwind's cost
and the report is held for five years.

## 4. Valuables

Jewellery and watches above £25,000 require a valuation less than three years old.
Fine art above £50,000 requires a schedule with provenance.

## 5. Security conditions

A risk with contents above £250,000 requires an alarm to EN50131 grade 2 with
remote signalling and a maintenance contract, applied as an endorsement.
