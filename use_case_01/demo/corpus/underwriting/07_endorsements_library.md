# Endorsements Library

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · reference UWE-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. How endorsements are applied

An endorsement is applied at quotation, appears on the schedule, and is repeated in
the renewal invitation. An endorsement added mid-term requires the customer's
agreement and is confirmed in writing.

## 2. NE01 Unoccupancy

Cover is limited to fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft and subsidence while the
premises are unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.

## 3. NE04 Water turned off

Between 1 November and 31 March the water supply must be turned off at the mains and
the system drained whenever the premises are unattended for more than 14 days, unless
the heating is left on at a minimum of 12 degrees Celsius.

## 4. NE07 Minimum security

The final exit door must be fitted with a five-lever mortice deadlock to BS3621 and
all accessible windows with key-operated locks, in use whenever the premises are
unattended.

## 5. NE12 Telematics

The telematics device must remain fitted and functioning. Cover may be cancelled with
14 days' notice where the device is removed, disabled or obstructed.

## 6. NE19 Flood resilience

Installed flood resilience measures must be maintained in working order and deployed
in accordance with the local flood warning service.
