# Motor Underwriting Guide

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · guide UWM-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Scope

Private car and small van business written through brokers. This guide sets the
appetite; it does not set price. Price comes from the rating engine.

## 2. Minimum age and licence

The minimum age for a proposer is 21 for standard rates. Drivers aged 17 to 20 are
acceptable only with a telematics policy and only on vehicles in group 1 to 15. A
full UK licence held for less than 12 months is referable in every case.

## 3. Young drivers on high-powered cars

A driver under 25 on a vehicle in insurance group 30 or above is outside appetite and
is declined, regardless of telematics, driving record or parental ownership. There is
no referral route for this and underwriters must not seek one.

## 4. Convictions

Up to two minor convictions (SP, CU, MS codes) with a total of six points are
acceptable at standard rates. Any IN10, DR or DD conviction, and any disqualification
in the last five years, is referred to a senior underwriter.

## 5. Vehicle modifications

Cosmetic modifications are acceptable if declared. Performance modifications --
engine, exhaust, forced induction, suspension, brakes -- are declined on any vehicle
where the driver is under 25 and referred otherwise.

## 6. Business use

Class 1 business use is acceptable. Class 2 and any commercial travelling use is
referred. Hire and reward, including food delivery and private hire, is declined on
this product.

## 7. Telematics

A telematics policy carries an endorsement requiring the device to remain fitted and
active. Persistent poor scores trigger the coaching process, not immediate
cancellation.
