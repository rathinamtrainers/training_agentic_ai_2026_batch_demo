# Home Underwriting Guide

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · guide UWH-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Scope

Buildings and contents cover for owner-occupied and let residential property written
through brokers.

## 2. Standard acceptance

Standard construction (brick or stone walls, slate or tile roof), built after 1900,
in good repair, owner-occupied, no more than three bedrooms above four, with a
rebuild cost up to £750,000, is acceptable without referral.

## 3. Non-standard construction

Thatch, timber frame built before 1960, prefabricated reinforced concrete, and any
flat roof exceeding 30% of the total roof area are referred. Prefabricated reinforced
concrete of the Cornish, Airey or Woolaway types is declined.

## 4. Previous subsidence

A property with a history of subsidence is acceptable only where remedial work is
complete, a certificate of structural adequacy has been issued, and there has been no
further movement for at least 10 years. Otherwise it is declined.

## 5. Unoccupancy

Properties unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days are referred and, if
accepted, carry an unoccupancy endorsement restricting cover to fire, lightning,
explosion, aircraft and subsidence.

## 6. Holiday and second homes

Second homes are acceptable with an endorsement requiring the water to be turned off
between 1 November and 31 March when the property is unattended for more than 14
days.

## 7. Sum insured

Rebuild costs are checked against the published index. A rebuild cost more than 25%
below the index figure for the property type is queried before quotation.
