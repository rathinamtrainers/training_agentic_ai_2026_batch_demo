# Flood Risk Appetite

> Northwind Assurance · Underwriting · policy UWF-02 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Flood zones

Northwind uses the four internal flood bands A to D derived from the national flood
map and the property's own history. Band A and B are within appetite at standard
rates.

## 2. Band C

Band C properties are acceptable with a flood excess of £2,500 and only where there
has been no flood claim in the last 10 years. The excess cannot be bought down.

## 3. Band D

Band D properties are outside appetite for new business. Existing Band D policies are
renewed only where the risk is eligible for the industry flood reinsurance scheme and
the broker has confirmed eligibility in writing.

## 4. Flood defences

Individual property flood resilience measures -- barriers, non-return valves, raised
electrics -- may move a Band C risk to standard terms on referral, with photographic
evidence and an installer's certificate.

## 5. Surface water

Surface water flooding from an inadequate drainage system serving the property is
treated as flood for appetite purposes even where the policy wording treats it
differently.
