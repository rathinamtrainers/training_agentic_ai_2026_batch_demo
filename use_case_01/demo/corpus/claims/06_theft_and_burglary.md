# Theft and Burglary Claims

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-12 · reviewed 2026-01-20

## 1. Crime reference

A theft claim is not progressed without a crime reference number from the police. The
handler asks for it at first contact and records it on the file.

## 2. Forcible and violent entry

Theft from the home is covered where there is evidence of forcible and violent entry
or exit, or where a person is present and threatened. Theft by deception at the door
is covered only under the Contents section optional extension.

## 3. Valuables limits

Unspecified valuables are covered to a total of £5,000 and no single unspecified item
is covered above £2,000. Items above that limit must have been specified on the
schedule before the loss.

## 4. Proof of ownership

Proof of ownership is requested proportionately: for items under £500 the customer's
description and any photograph is normally enough. Demanding receipts for ordinary
household items is a complaint waiting to happen.

## 5. Settlement

Settlement is on a new-for-old basis for contents unless the schedule says otherwise.
Where a replacement is sourced through a Northwind supplier, the customer may take a
cash settlement instead at the price Northwind would have paid.
