# Escape of Water — Northwind Home Policy Wording (extract)

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · policy wording NWH-2026 v4.2 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. What this section covers

This section applies to the Buildings and Contents sections of the Northwind Home
policy. It is the wording a claims handler reads out when a customer reports water
damage, and it is the wording quoted in a settlement letter.

## 2. Definitions

"Escape of water" means water escaping from any fixed water or heating installation,
fixed domestic appliance, or fixed water tank, apparatus or pipe. Rainwater entering
through a defective roof is NOT escape of water; it is dealt with under storm damage.

## 3. Escape of water within the insured premises

We cover damage caused by an escape of water from a fixed installation within the
insured premises. We also pay the reasonable cost of removing and replacing any part
of the building necessary to find and repair the source of the leak, up to £5,000 in
any one claim. We do not pay for the repair of the failed appliance or pipe itself.

## 4. Escape of water from a neighbouring flat or property

Damage to the insured premises caused by an escape of water originating in a
neighbouring flat, maisonette or adjoining property IS covered under this section,
whether or not the neighbour is insured and whether or not the neighbour was
negligent. The claim is settled under the insured's own policy first and Northwind
then pursues recovery from the responsible party where there is a reasonable
prospect of it. The standard escape of water excess of £350 applies. Cover is not
withheld because the source of the leak is outside the insured's control.

## 5. Exclusions

We do not cover damage caused by water escaping while the premises have been
unoccupied for more than 45 consecutive days unless the water was turned off at the
mains and the system drained, or the heating was left on at a minimum of 12 degrees
Celsius between 1 November and 31 March. We do not cover gradually operating causes,
including damage from a slow leak that a reasonable person would have noticed.

## 6. Excess

The escape of water excess is £350 for buildings damage and £100 for contents damage,
and only one excess is applied where a single incident damages both.

## 7. Trace and access

Where the source of the leak cannot be found without removing part of the structure,
the trace and access limit in clause 3 applies. Trace and access costs are not
payable where no escape of water is subsequently found.
