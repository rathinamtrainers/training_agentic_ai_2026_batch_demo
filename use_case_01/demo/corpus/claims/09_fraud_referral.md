# Fraud Referral

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-20 · reviewed 2026-05-05

## 1. When to refer

A claim is referred to the counter-fraud team where two or more of the published
indicators are present, or where the handler has a specific reason to doubt the
claim. A hunch on its own is recorded but is not a referral.

## 2. What the handler must not do

The handler does not accuse, does not tell the customer a referral has been made, and
does not delay a decision without saying that the claim is under investigation. A
claim held silently is a complaint and often an Ombudsman case.

## 3. Timescales

The counter-fraud team responds to a referral within five working days with one of:
release, request further evidence, or take over the file.

## 4. Repudiation

A claim is declined for fraud only on the counter-fraud team's written recommendation
and with the technical claims manager's approval, and the letter states the specific
policy condition relied upon.

## 5. Recording

All referrals and outcomes are recorded on the industry register in line with the
fair processing notice given to the customer at inception.
