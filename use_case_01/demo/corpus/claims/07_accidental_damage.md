# Accidental Damage Claims

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-14 · reviewed 2026-04-11

## 1. What accidental damage is

Sudden, unexpected and visible damage caused by something external and unintended.
It is an optional extension on the Northwind Home policy and is not included as
standard on Buildings or Contents.

## 2. Checking the schedule first

Before liability is discussed the handler checks whether accidental damage cover was
purchased. Telling a customer they are covered and correcting it later is the single
worst thing this team does.

## 3. What is always excluded

Damage caused by wear and tear, by pets, by insects or vermin, by chewing, scratching
or tearing, and any damage occurring gradually, is excluded whether or not accidental
damage cover was purchased.

## 4. Common accepted examples

Paint spilt on a carpet, a foot through a ceiling, a television knocked over, a
worktop cracked by a dropped pan. These are settled at first contact where the value
is under £500 and the description is consistent.

## 5. Matching sets

Where one item of a matching set is damaged beyond repair, Northwind pays for the
damaged item only. There is no undamaged-pair cover on this policy.
