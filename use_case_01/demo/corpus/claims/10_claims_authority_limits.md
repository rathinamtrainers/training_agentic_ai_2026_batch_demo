# Claims Authority Limits

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · reference CLM-03 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Handler authority

A claims handler may authorise settlement up to £5,000 on any one claim, and may
authorise an interim payment up to £2,000, without referral.

## 2. Senior handler authority

A senior handler may authorise up to £25,000, and may agree a commercial settlement
up to £2,500 where liability is arguable.

## 3. Team leader authority

A team leader may authorise up to £100,000 and is the first level at which a claim
may be declined on liability grounds.

## 4. Technical claims manager

Settlements above £100,000, all fraud repudiations, and any decision to decline a
claim where the customer has already complained, require the technical claims
manager.

## 5. Ex gratia payments

An ex gratia payment where there is no policy liability requires a team leader up to
£500 and the technical claims manager above that. The reason is recorded as service
failure, goodwill, or vulnerability, and the classification feeds the Complaints
team's root cause reporting.

## 6. Recording authority

Every authorisation is recorded in Polaris against the authoriser's own identity.
Shared logins are prohibited without exception.
