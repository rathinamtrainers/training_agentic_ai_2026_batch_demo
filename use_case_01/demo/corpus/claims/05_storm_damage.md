# Storm Damage Claims

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-09 · reviewed 2026-02-14

## 1. Definition of a storm

A storm means wind speeds of at least 47 knots (54 mph), or torrential rainfall of at
least 25mm per hour, or snow to a depth of at least 30cm in 24 hours, or hail of such
intensity that it damages hard surfaces. Weather data is obtained for the postcode
and the date before liability is decided.

## 2. Wear and tear

Storm cover does not extend to damage that a storm merely revealed. A roof already in
poor repair is not made a storm claim by the wind that finally lifted it, and the
handler must say so plainly and early rather than at the end of an investigation.

## 3. Fences, gates and hedges

Damage to fences, gates and hedges by storm is excluded under the Northwind Home
policy. This exclusion causes more complaints than any other single wording, so it is
explained at first contact.

## 4. Emergency repairs

The customer may authorise emergency work to make the property safe and watertight up
to £1,000 without prior approval, and must keep the invoice and photographs.

## 5. Surge of claims

During a named storm event the Claims team moves to the surge process: acknowledgement
within one working day, triage by severity, and a target of first contact within five
working days rather than two.
