# Subsidence, Heave and Landslip Claims

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-11 · reviewed 2026-03-02

## 1. Scope

Damage to the buildings caused by subsidence, heave or landslip of the site on which
the buildings stand. Damage to paths, drives, walls, gates and fences is covered only
where the main building is damaged at the same time and by the same cause.

## 2. Excess

The subsidence excess is £1,000 and is applied to every subsidence claim regardless
of the cause. It is the highest excess in the Northwind Home policy and is stated on
the schedule.

## 3. Investigation

Every subsidence notification is referred to an engineer for site investigation
within 10 working days. The investigation typically includes trial pits, drainage
testing and a level survey.

## 4. Monitoring

Where movement is suspected to be seasonal, the property is monitored for a minimum
of 12 months before repairs are authorised. The customer is told the timescale in
writing at the point monitoring starts.

## 5. Tree removal

Where vegetation is the proximate cause, removal or pruning is arranged with the
owner's consent. Where the tree is on a neighbour's land or is subject to a
preservation order, the case is referred to the technical claims manager.
