# Excesses and Settlement

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · reference CLM-02 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. Standard excesses

Buildings £250. Contents £100. Escape of water £350 buildings and £100 contents.
Subsidence £1,000. Accidental damage £250. Motor comprehensive £350 plus any
voluntary excess shown on the schedule.

## 2. One excess per incident

Where a single incident causes damage under more than one section, only the highest
applicable excess is charged. This is applied automatically in Polaris and is checked
by the handler before payment.

## 3. Payment methods

Settlement is by bank transfer to the policyholder's account within five working days
of agreement. Cheques are issued only where no account details can be verified.

## 4. Interim payments

Where a claim will take more than 30 days to settle and the customer is out of
pocket, an interim payment of up to 50% of the agreed minimum liability is made.

## 5. Under-insurance

Where the sum insured is less than the full rebuild or replacement cost, settlement is
reduced in proportion. Average is applied only where the shortfall exceeds 15%, and
the calculation is shown to the customer in writing.
