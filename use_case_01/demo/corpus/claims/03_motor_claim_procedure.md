# Motor Claim Procedure

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · procedure CLM-07 · reviewed 2026-05-18

## 1. Notification

Motor claims are notified through the 24-hour claims line or the broker portal. The
handler records the registration, the date and time of the incident, the location,
and whether anyone was injured, before anything else.

## 2. Injury claims

Any claim involving a personal injury is referred to the injury team on the day of
notification and is never handled by the general motor desk.

## 3. Third party intervention

Where the third party is not at fault and has not yet instructed a credit hire
company, the handler offers Northwind's own mobility and repair service within four
hours of notification. Intervention after 48 hours is usually too late and the
credit hire costs follow.

## 4. Approved repairers

Repairs carried out through a Northwind approved repairer carry a five-year
guarantee on workmanship. A customer using their own repairer must supply two
estimates where the repair is estimated above £2,500.

## 5. Total loss

A vehicle is declared a total loss where the estimated repair cost exceeds 60% of
its pre-accident market value. The pre-accident value is evidenced from two trade
guides and the higher figure is used.

## 6. Uninsured loss recovery

Where the third party is liable, the customer's uninsured losses including their
policy excess are pursued as part of the recovery. The customer does not pay for
this service.
