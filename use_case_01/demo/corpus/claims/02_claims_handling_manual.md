# Claims Handling Manual — service standards

> Northwind Assurance · Claims · internal manual CLM-01 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. Purpose

The service standards every Northwind claims handler works to. These are internal
commitments, not policy terms, and are what the Claims team is measured on.

## 2. First contact

A new claim notification must be acknowledged on the same working day it is
received. The handler must make first substantive contact with the customer within
two working days of notification, and record the contact on the claim file in
Polaris.

## 3. Setting a reserve

An initial reserve is set at first contact, based on the handler's best estimate of
the total cost of the claim including fees. A reserve is never left at zero after
first contact. Reserves are reviewed at every material development and at least
every 30 days.

## 4. Keeping the customer updated

The customer receives an update at least every 10 working days while the claim is
open, even where the update is that nothing has changed. Silence is the single
largest driver of complaints against the Claims team.

## 5. Appointing a loss adjuster

A loss adjuster is appointed where the estimated claim value exceeds £15,000, where
the cause of loss is disputed, or where the handler suspects the claim is not what
it appears to be. The appointment is recorded with a reason.

## 6. Closing a claim

A claim is closed only when payment has been made and cleared, or the claim has been
declined in writing with the reason and the complaints route stated. A claim is never
closed for inactivity.

## 7. Escalation

Anything the handler cannot settle within their authority under
10_claims_authority_limits.md goes to a team leader the same day, with the file note
explaining what is needed.
