# Complaints Handling Procedure

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · procedure CMP-01 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. What is a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, whether justified or not, about
Northwind's provision of a financial service, where the complainant is a customer or
is acting on a customer's behalf. It does not have to use the word complaint, and
staff must not ask the customer to put it in writing before it is logged.

## 2. Logging

Every complaint is logged in ServiceDesk on the day it is received, with the date it
was first made to anyone at Northwind or to the broker. That date starts the
regulatory clock, not the date it reached the Complaints team.

## 3. Acknowledgement

A written acknowledgement is sent within three business days of receipt, naming the
handler and explaining how the complaint will be handled.

## 4. Final response

A final response is sent within eight weeks of the date the complaint was received.
The final response states the outcome, the reasons for it, any redress offered, and
the customer's right to refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within
six months.

## 5. Where the complaint is resolved quickly

A complaint resolved to the customer's satisfaction by close of business on the third
business day may be answered with a summary resolution communication instead of a
full final response, and that communication must still mention the Financial
Ombudsman Service.

## 6. If eight weeks cannot be met

Where a final response cannot be issued within eight weeks, a holding letter is sent
before the eight weeks expire, explaining why and telling the customer they may refer
the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service now.

## 7. Tone

The final response is written in plain English, addresses what the customer actually
complained about, and does not defend the decision at the expense of answering the
question.
