# Complaint Records and Retention

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · policy CMP-18 · reviewed 2026-04-30

## 1. What is kept

The complaint as received, the acknowledgement, all correspondence, the investigation
notes, the final response, and the redress calculation.

## 2. Retention period

Complaint files are retained for three years from the date of the final response.
Files referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service are retained for six years.

## 3. Data protection

A complaint file is personal data. Access is limited to the Complaints team and to
those investigating, and every access is logged.

## 4. Subject access

A subject access request touching a complaint file is answered within one month by the
data protection officer, with the Complaints team supplying the file within five
working days of being asked.

## 5. Deletion

Deletion at the end of the retention period is automatic and evidenced. Nothing is
deleted while an Ombudsman case, litigation or a regulatory request is open.
