# Goodwill and Redress Payments

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · reference CMP-08 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Redress and goodwill are different

Redress puts the customer back in the position they would have been in. Goodwill
recognises distress and inconvenience. A final response says which is being offered
and why.

## 2. Authority limits

A complaints handler may authorise up to £500. A complaints team leader may authorise
up to £2,500. Above £2,500 requires the head of customer relations. Any payment above
£10,000 requires the chief operating officer.

## 3. Distress and inconvenience bands

Up to £100 for minor inconvenience resolved quickly. £100 to £300 where the customer
had to chase repeatedly. £300 to £750 where the failure caused real distress over
weeks. Above £750 where the impact was substantial or prolonged, and the rationale is
written on the file.

## 4. Interest

Where money was wrongly withheld, simple interest at 8% a year is added from the date
the customer should have had it to the date of payment.

## 5. Payments are never conditional

A goodwill payment is never made conditional on the customer withdrawing the complaint
or agreeing not to go to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
