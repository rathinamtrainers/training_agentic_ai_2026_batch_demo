# The Escalation Pack

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · reference CMP-21 · reviewed 2026-06-15

## 1. When a pack is built

When a complaint is escalated to the head of customer relations, when it is referred
to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and when it is likely to attract press or
regulatory attention.

## 2. Contents

A one-page chronology, the complaint and the final response, the policy documents in
force at the time, the claim or policy record, the redress calculation, and the root
cause entry.

## 3. The chronology

Dates and facts only. No opinion, no defence and no adjectives. A chronology that
argues is a chronology nobody trusts.

## 4. Who signs it off

The complaints team leader checks the pack for completeness and the head of customer
relations signs it before it leaves Northwind.

## 5. Timescale

A pack is built within five working days of the trigger, and within the Ombudsman's
own deadline where that is shorter.
