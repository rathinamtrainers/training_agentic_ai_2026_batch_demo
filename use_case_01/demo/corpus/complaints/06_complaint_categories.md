# Complaint Categories

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · reference CMP-11 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. Why categories matter

The category drives the regulatory return, the root cause report and the board pack.
A complaint miscategorised is a trend nobody sees.

## 2. The categories

Claim outcome. Claim delay. Claim service. Pricing at renewal. Sales and advice.
Administration and documentation. Broker service. Data and privacy.

## 3. Choosing one

The category is what the customer is unhappy about, not which department caused it.
A delay caused by underwriting on a claim is a claim delay.

## 4. Secondary categories

One secondary category may be recorded where two distinct failures occurred. Three or
more is a sign the file needs splitting into separate complaints.

## 5. Reporting

Categories are reported monthly to the board and half-yearly to the regulator in the
prescribed format.
