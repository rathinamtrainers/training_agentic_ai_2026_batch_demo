# Vulnerable Customers

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · policy CMP-06 · reviewed 2026-04-18

## 1. What vulnerability means here

A customer who, because of their personal circumstances, is especially susceptible to
harm. The four drivers are health, life events, resilience and capability. Anyone at
Northwind may record a vulnerability; it is not a specialist judgement.

## 2. Recording

A vulnerability is recorded with the customer's consent, in their own words where
possible, and is reviewed rather than treated as permanent.

## 3. Adjustments

Adjustments include more time to respond, a named contact, communication in another
format, accepting a third party's authority, and pausing recovery action.

## 4. Bereavement

Bereavement complaints are handled by a named handler from receipt to close, and
never pass between handlers on a rota.

## 5. Time limits

Internal timescales may be extended for a vulnerable customer where that helps them.
Regulatory timescales are not extended, and the eight-week final response deadline
still applies.
