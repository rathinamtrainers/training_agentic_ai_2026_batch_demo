# Complaints Reporting to the Board

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · reference CMP-25 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. Monthly pack

Volumes by category and by team, uphold rate, average days to final response,
Ombudsman referrals and outcomes, redress paid, and the top three root causes with
their owners.

## 2. The number the board watches

Complaints per thousand policies in force, split by product. Absolute volumes move
with the book and mislead.

## 3. Ombudsman uphold rate

Reported against the published industry average. A rate above the average for two
consecutive quarters triggers a review by the audit committee.

## 4. Half-yearly regulatory return

Submitted in the prescribed format within 30 business days of the reporting period
end, and reconciled to the monthly packs before submission.

## 5. What must never be netted off

Complaints resolved within three business days are reported separately and are never
netted off the headline volume to make a trend look better.
