# Financial Ombudsman Service Referrals

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · procedure CMP-04 · reviewed 2026-05-22

## 1. The customer's right

A customer may refer a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within six months
of the date of our final response. The right and the deadline are stated in every
final response, and the Ombudsman's own leaflet is enclosed.

## 2. When the Ombudsman contacts us

The Complaints team responds to an Ombudsman case file request within the timescale
the Ombudsman sets, and always within 14 days where no timescale is given.

## 3. The case file

The file contains the complaint, the final response, the policy documents in force,
all correspondence, the claim or policy record, and a chronology. Nothing is removed
from a file sent to the Ombudsman.

## 4. Settling before a decision

Northwind may settle a case before the Ombudsman decides where the review shows the
complaint should have been upheld. Settling to avoid an adverse decision on a point
that will recur is not permitted; that point is fixed instead.

## 5. Case fees

Each case referred incurs a case fee whether or not it is upheld. The fee is reported
monthly to the board alongside the uphold rate.

## 6. Learning

Every Ombudsman outcome, upheld or not, goes to root cause analysis under
07_root_cause_analysis.md.
