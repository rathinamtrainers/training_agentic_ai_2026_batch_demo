# Complaint Timescales at a Glance

> Northwind Assurance · Complaints · reference CMP-02 · reviewed 2026-06-30

## 1. Day zero

The day the complaint was first made to anyone at Northwind, or to the broker acting
for Northwind. Not the day it reached the Complaints team.

## 2. Three business days

Summary resolution communication where the complaint is resolved, or a written
acknowledgement where it is not.

## 3. Four weeks

An update to the customer where the complaint is still open, saying what has been done
and what remains.

## 4. Eight weeks

The final response, or a holding letter explaining the delay and giving the customer
the right to go to the Financial Ombudsman Service now.

## 5. Six months

The period the customer has, from our final response, to refer the complaint to the
Financial Ombudsman Service.

## 6. Three years

The period a complaint file is retained after closure under
08_records_and_retention.md.
