# Broker Commission Terms

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRC-01 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Standard commission rates

Commission is paid on net premium excluding insurance premium tax and any fees, at
the following standard rates: private motor 12.5%, home 20%, small commercial
combined 22.5%, and commercial motor 15%.

## 2. Renewal commission

Renewal commission is paid at the same rate as new business on every product except
private motor, where renewal commission is 10%. There is no reduction in later
renewal years.

## 3. Enhanced commission

An enhanced rate of up to 25% on home and 25% on small commercial is available to
agencies writing more than £250,000 of gross written premium in a scheme year, agreed
in writing by the head of distribution and reviewed annually.

## 4. Payment terms

Commission is retained by the broker from the premium collected and settled net under
the credit terms in 03_agency_terms_and_credit.md. Northwind does not pay commission
separately by invoice.

## 5. Fees charged by the broker

Broker fees charged to the customer are the broker's own and are not shared with
Northwind, but must be disclosed to the customer before inception in line with the
Terms of Business Agreement.

## 6. Commission on mid-term adjustments

Commission is payable on additional premium at the same rate as the original
transaction, and is clawed back proportionately on return premium.
