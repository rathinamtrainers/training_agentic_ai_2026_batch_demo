# Broker Onboarding

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · procedure BRO-01 · reviewed 2026-06-01

## 1. What is required

A completed agency application, the firm's FCA reference number, evidence of
professional indemnity cover of at least £1,750,000, the last two years of audited
accounts, and details of the client money arrangement.

## 2. Terms of Business Agreement

No business is accepted before a signed Terms of Business Agreement is held. A
quotation may be provided during onboarding, but it cannot be bound.

## 3. Due diligence

Broker Support checks the FCA register, the firm's permissions, the directors against
sanctions lists, and any adverse credit. Checks are repeated annually.

## 4. Timescales

A complete application is decided within 10 working days. An incomplete application is
returned within three working days naming exactly what is missing.

## 5. Agency numbers

On approval the agency receives a Northwind agency number and portal credentials
within two working days. Credentials are issued to named individuals only and are
never shared at firm level.
