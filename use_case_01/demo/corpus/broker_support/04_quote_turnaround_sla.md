# Quote and Service Turnaround

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRS-04 · reviewed 2026-06-01

## 1. New business quotations

A complete new business submission within appetite is quoted within one working day.
A submission requiring underwriter referral is answered within two working days.

## 2. Mid-term adjustments

Adjustments keyed on the portal are immediate. Adjustments requiring referral are
completed within two working days.

## 3. Documentation

Policy documents are issued electronically within one working day of inception.
Paper documents, where requested, are posted within five working days.

## 4. Telephone service

The Broker Support line is open 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday. The target is to
answer 85% of calls within 30 seconds and to abandon fewer than 3%.

## 5. What is not covered by this standard

Claims enquiries are handled by the Claims team on their own service standards and are
not measured here. Broker Support does not give claims decisions.
