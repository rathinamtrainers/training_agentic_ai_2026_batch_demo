# New Business Submission Standards

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRN-02 · reviewed 2026-05-20

## 1. A complete submission

Proposer details, full risk details, five years of claims experience, current insurer
and expiry date, and any referral information the appetite guide asks for.

## 2. Claims experience

Claims experience is required for all commercial business and for any home or motor
risk with a claim in the last five years. A statement of "no claims" without a period
stated is not claims experience.

## 3. Presentation of risk

The broker presents a fair presentation of the risk under the Insurance Act 2015.
Broker Support does not underwrite; it checks completeness only.

## 4. Duplicate submissions

The same risk submitted through two channels is quoted once, on the first submission
received. The second is closed and the broker is told which reference stands.

## 5. Quote validity

A quotation is valid for 30 days from issue, or until the risk changes, whichever is
sooner.
