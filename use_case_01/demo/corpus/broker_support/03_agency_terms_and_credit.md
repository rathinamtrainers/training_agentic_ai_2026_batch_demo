# Agency Terms and Credit

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRA-02 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Credit period

Premium is settled to Northwind within 45 days of the end of the month of inception,
renewal or adjustment. This is the standard credit period for all agencies.

## 2. Statements

A statement is issued on the first working day of each month showing transactions,
commission retained and the balance due.

## 3. Overdue accounts

An account more than 15 days beyond the credit period is placed on stop for new
business. Existing policies are not cancelled for a broker's debt; the customer is
not the debtor.

## 4. Return premiums

Return premiums are offset against the next statement rather than refunded
separately, unless the agency is closing.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate the agency on 90 days' written notice. On termination the
broker retains renewal rights for 12 months, and Northwind does not contact the
customers directly during that period except for regulatory correspondence.
