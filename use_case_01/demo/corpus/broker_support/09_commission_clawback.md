# Commission Clawback

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRC-04 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. When commission is clawed back

Commission is clawed back in full where a policy is cancelled from inception, where a
policy is voided for non-disclosure, and where premium is not collected.

## 2. Cancellation within the cooling-off period

Where the customer cancels within the 14-day cooling-off period, commission is clawed
back in full and no broker fee is recoverable from Northwind.

## 3. Mid-term cancellation

On a mid-term cancellation after the cooling-off period, commission is clawed back
proportionately to the return premium.

## 4. Claims made

Where a claim has been made in the current period of insurance, no return premium is
due on cancellation and there is therefore no clawback.

## 5. Disputes

A clawback dispute is raised with Broker Support within 60 days of the statement on
which it appears, and is answered within 10 working days.
