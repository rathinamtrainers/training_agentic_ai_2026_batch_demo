# Delegated Authority Schemes

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRD-03 · effective 2026-04-01

## 1. What is delegated

A small number of agencies hold binding authority for defined home and small
commercial products, within a written scheme agreement and a bordereau requirement.

## 2. Bordereau

The bordereau is submitted by the tenth working day of each month in the Northwind
template. A late or non-conforming bordereau suspends the authority until it is
corrected.

## 3. Audit

Every delegated authority is audited annually, and within the first six months for a
new scheme. Audit findings are graded and a red finding suspends binding immediately.

## 4. Outside appetite

A delegated authority never extends to a risk the appetite guide declines. Binding
such a risk is a breach of the scheme agreement and the policy is still honoured to
the customer while the breach is dealt with between Northwind and the agency.

## 5. Claims

Claims handling is never delegated. All claims under scheme business are handled by
the Northwind Claims team.
