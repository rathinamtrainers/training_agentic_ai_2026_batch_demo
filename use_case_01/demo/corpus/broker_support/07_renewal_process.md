# Renewal Process

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · procedure BRR-01 · reviewed 2026-06-10

## 1. Renewal invitations

Renewal invitations are issued to the broker 28 days before expiry for personal lines
and 45 days before expiry for commercial.

## 2. Price transparency

Personal lines renewal documents show last year's premium alongside this year's, as
required by the pricing rules. Broker Support does not remove or reformat that
disclosure on request.

## 3. Auto-renewal

Auto-renewal applies where the customer has consented, and the consent is recorded by
the broker. A customer who has not consented is not auto-renewed.

## 4. Changes at renewal

Any change to terms, excesses or endorsements at renewal is highlighted in the
invitation. An unhighlighted change is treated as a service failure and reported to
the Complaints team.

## 5. Late renewal

Cover cannot be backdated after expiry. A policy renewed after expiry incepts from the
date it is completed, and there is a gap the customer must be told about in writing.
