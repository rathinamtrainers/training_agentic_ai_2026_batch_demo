# Where a Broker Sends a Complaint

> Northwind Assurance · Broker Support · reference BRQ-01 · reviewed 2026-06-01

## 1. Broker Support does not handle complaints

A complaint from a customer, or from a broker on a customer's behalf, is passed to
the Complaints team the same day. Broker Support does not investigate, does not offer
redress, and does not tell the customer what the outcome will be.

## 2. How to pass it on

The complaint is logged in ServiceDesk with the policy number, the date the complaint
was made to the broker, and what the customer is asking for. The date the broker
first received it is the date the regulatory clock starts.

## 3. Complaints about the broker

Where the complaint is about the broker's own service rather than about Northwind, it
stays with the broker under their own procedure, and Broker Support records that this
was explained.

## 4. Complaints about Broker Support itself

A broker unhappy with Broker Support raises it with the head of distribution. This is
a relationship matter and not a regulated customer complaint.

## 5. What brokers ask for most

The two most common requests are a status update and a copy of the final response
letter. Both come from the Complaints team, not from this one.
